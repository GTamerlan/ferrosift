Both identified model checkpoints were evaluated on the same conveyor images. The labels make successes and misses reviewable.

01

Development scope

1,522 conveyor frames, 7,535 copper and 36,563 steel annotations. The model and operating point were selected using this set. Repeated appearances are separate annotations, not unique physical pieces. Six of 1,528 official validation frames lacked acquired labels and were excluded.

02

Our trained model and the reference

Ferrosift’s custom-trained vision model is fine-tuned from pretrained YOLO26s. The YOLO11s reference provides a reproducible same-image comparison. Architecture and training both differ; isolating the effect of custom training requires a future matched YOLO26s baseline.

Frozen settings, matching and source credits

Candidate copper threshold: 0.8073331713676453. Reference: 0.9101877808570862. Both use 640 px inputs, common class-aware NMS (IoU 0.7, maximum 1,000 detections, base confidence 0.001), FP32 and batch size 1. Historical alarm routing uses class-blind greedy matching at IoU ≥ 0.5. These counts are not standard AP or precision.

The selection budget is (steel matches + unmatched copper alarms) / steel annotations ≤ 1%. Ferrosift: (361 + 3) / 36,563; reference: (364 + 1) / 36,563. This annotation-level alarm-burden ratio is not a conventional false-positive rate, physical steel loss or copper mass recovery. This development comparison does not establish plant performance.
