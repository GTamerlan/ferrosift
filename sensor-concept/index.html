The question to test

Does keeping the whole response help?

Our hypothesis is that complementary field directions, full response sequences and explicit consistency checks may help classify layered PCB fragments. Optical geometry and pose would condition expectations; disagreement would increase uncertainty and support a recheck.

A matched comparison

Compare the proposed sequence method with the same sensors reduced to peak spectra, then with RGB-only, induction-only and RGB plus induction baselines. Test optional 3D as a separate addition.

A useful result must transfer

Hold out physical fragments and source lots. Vary pose, gap, overlap and belt background. Measure classification and recheck rates at matched false-removal limits before considering a material trial.

Technical scope, assumptions and research context

Experimental options: adjustable station positions and sensor gap; alternate head directions; an excitation schedule chosen from f₁ … fN after characterisation. Future engineering teams will compare these options on identified physical fragments.

This is a simplified schematic of the proposed one-lane retrofit-module experiment, not a dimensional rendering of the module CAD. The module CAD illustrates a six-winding bench arrangement with candidate balanced receivers; this simplified sequence drawing does not define the final coil count. A 180 mm mechanical lane on a 600 mm host belt is not validated coverage. Head dimensions, spacing, frequency schedule, shielding, field distribution, cross-talk, timing budget and performance are unvalidated. Field arrows indicate intended dominant directions, not a field solution.

Tx drives excitation; Rx records the electromagnetic response during each frequency window. This illustration proposes time-multiplexed acquisition; drive scheduling and cross-talk remain to be tested. Encoder belt travel and timestamps propose an association with P17; actual fragment motion can differ through slip, bounce or pose change. Optical updates and association uncertainty remain research requirements.

The actual belt reinforcement, frame, rollers and fasteners must be included in metal-interference tests. The exclusion envelope is an experimental reservation, not a proven shield or compatibility guarantee.

Induction responses depend on geometry, conductivity, permeability, thickness and sensor gap. The proposed output is a classification likelihood with uncertainty, not elemental composition, an internal copper map or a measured recovery claim.

How this builds on published work

Induction arrays, multiple frequencies, geometry-aware interpretation and multiple coil views have established research foundations. Revalis's proposed contribution is a specific PCB-composite experiment and its evaluation protocol.

ShredderSort · 2018

Combined multifrequency induction with structured-laser geometry. Its 16-channel array used three diagonal rows; the study evaluated one channel and reduced each frequency response to its maximum-magnitude sample.

Revalis question: Does retaining the complete passage response from two intended sensing directions improve PCB-composite decisions beyond the same data reduced to peak spectra?

Aluminium sensor comparison · 2024
