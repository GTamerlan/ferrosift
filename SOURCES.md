## Matched comparison and illustrative economics

Official vendor descriptions checked on 15 October 2026 establish existing capabilities: [STEINERT KSS CLI](https://steinertglobal.com/us/sorting-systems/sensor-sorting/combined-sensor-sorting-systems/steinert-kss-cli-1/) combines colour, laser 3D and induction; [TOMRA’s 14 April 2026 FINDER announcement](https://www.tomra.com/en-gb/waste-metal-recycling/media-center/news/2026/tomra-recycling-launches-the-new-finder) describes PCB sorting and insulated-wire recovery. These sources do not establish that competitors lack multiple sensing directions. Revalis must demonstrate useful incremental information and better routing against strong simpler baselines and the incumbent process at matched throughput, output purity and total cost.

The concrete two-bin example comes from [Koinig et al., Processes 14(5), 746 (2026), Section 3.6](https://www.mdpi.com/2227-9717/14/5/746). The trial used an RGB camera, computer vision and pneumatic air-jet ejection; output streams were manually sorted and weighed. It does not test multi-direction induction. The nominal 490 kg run reported 490.92 kg of outputs: 452.98 kg accepted (including 0.42 kg visibly copper-bearing objects) and 37.94 kg rejected (3.55 kg copper-bearing objects, 33.84 kg iron and 0.55 kg other material). Missed copper-bearing-object mass is 0.42/(3.55+0.42) × 100 = 10.5793450882%, rounded to 10.6%. Iron diversion is 33.84/490.92 × 100 = 6.89318015155% of the whole measured batch, rounded to 6.9%. The denominators differ and the rates must not be added. These are whole-object masses, not elemental copper. The 99.907% headline is visible-object mass purity, not chemical analysis. The paper reports some iron-wire errors but does not quantify every cause of iron diversion. The reject fraction retained industrial copper-smelter value. One feed and trial do not establish industry-wide or all-e-waste inefficiency.

The separate illustrative target assumes 50% less iron diversion with unchanged copper-bearing-object rejection: 33.84 × 0.5 = 16.92 kg retained iron in the same batch. Normalization uses all measured outputs, 490.92 kg: 33.84/490.92 × 0.5 × 1,000 = 34.4659007578 kg retained iron/t. At an assumed 10,000 t/year of compatible feed and an assumed US$100/t incremental value spread between the preferred and reject destinations, (33.84/490.92 × 0.5) × 10,000 × US$100 = US$34,465.9007578/year, or about US$34,466 illustrative gross value before costs. Both streams retain value. Feed volume, improvement and spread are assumptions, not rated capacity, a market quote or measured Revalis performance. Equipment, installation, operation, maintenance, energy, financing and other added costs could erase the gross value. No CV score is converted into recovery or money. Annual arithmetic is inside an expandable disclosure; the original operator calculator is preserved separately.

## Module rationale and longer horizon

The public methods section links primary research on moving-belt induction, optical/inductive fusion and controlled PCB inspection, plus incumbent vendor descriptions. Generic sensor fusion already exists. Revalis proposes testing geometry-conditioned directional and frequency response sequences on independent PCB/e-scrap lots against strong simpler baselines. No source validates the exact proposed module or establishes direct elemental assay.

The homepage's orientation hypothesis is consistent with [Abdel Rehim et al.'s directional measurement study](https://pure.manchester.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/47916570/FULL_TEXT.PDF), already linked in the Explorer, and [Ledger and Lionheart's primary-author analysis](https://arxiv.org/html/1906.00382v1), which gives the rotation rule for a magnetic polarizability tensor (§7). [Zhao et al.'s in-line scanning study](https://research.manchester.ac.uk/en/publications/determining-the-electromagnetic-polarizability-tensors-of-metal-o-2/) also identifies the dependence on coil geometry and measurement protocol. These support the statement that orientation can change a fragment's response. They do not establish an orientation-loss percentage, complete tensor recovery from the proposed two field directions, unique copper identification, or improved classification by this module. More reliable classification remains a hypothesis to test.

The 2045 horizon is aspirational. Published magnetic manipulation and detumbling work describes constrained laboratory experiments or simulations, not Revalis flight evidence. Any orbital application would require a separate actuation architecture, stand-off/force/torque testing, power and thermal engineering, reaction control, safe proximity operations, a mission partner and flight qualification. Detumbling concerns relative rotation, not stopping orbital motion. The dated, source-specific boundaries and accessible primary links are on the homepage under Sources.

Their appearance does not encode signal amplitude. The actual coil geometry remains unchanged. ## Beginner guide

The optional beginner guide uses original schematics and captions. The induction explanation is grounded in [Feynman Lectures II–17](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/II_17.html) and [OpenStax University Physics 13.5](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/13-5-eddy-currents): changing magnetic fields can drive circulating electric fields and currents in conductors, which alter a receiving coil's electrical response. The guide is not a field solution, interior elemental map, tracking demonstration or measured sorting result. Its record association and routing sequence describe the proposed Revalis architecture.

The circulating currents are induced within conductive scrap; they do not enter it from the air or cover. The proposed nonconductive sensing window is distinct from a metal shield. The receiver/readout inset shows a function, not a coil location or orientation. The directional studies above also explain why response depends on geometry: moving or rotating the same piece can change its response without changing its copper content, and a stronger response alone is not a copper assay. Optional exterior height/pose sensing may help account for distance and orientation, subject to calibration with known samples and testing; it does not reveal hidden metal depth or provide automatic calibration. The proposed combination of appearance, optional surface geometry and directional induction informs routing, not an internal elemental map. A piece ID is a software label; encoder and tracking association must link the readings to that piece, and the label alone does not establish correct association.

## Industry evidence

The 2024 Global E-waste Monitor from UNITAR / ITU reports 2022 estimates of 62 million tonnes generated, 22.3% documented formal collection/recycling, and US$62 billion in recoverable natural resources left unaccounted for. The homepage computes the complement (77.7%) and rounds the two corresponding tonnages (13.8 and 48.2 million). The mass and resource-value figures are separate measures, not a units conversion. The resource value is not the value Revalis could recover. These are not audited company losses, and the complement is not all landfill.

https://www.itu.int/en/mediacentre/Pages/PR-2024-03-20-e-waste-recycling.aspx

TOMRA's manufacturer-published customer case studies describe PNW Metal's loss of smaller metals to landfill and expanded Zorba product offerings, and Premier Surplus's approximately 95% first-pass material recovery. Product offerings growing fourfold is not a claim of fourfold recovery. Neither source provides a dollar value for company losses. These examples are unrelated to Revalis’s own performance. In the Premier article, the 75–90% figure is a consultant expectation; about 95% is the operator's report for its specific e-scrap line, downstream of a Javelin eddy-current separator. Neither is a universal baseline or multilayer-PCB copper-recovery rate. The hypothetical operator example uses independent assumptions. Further processing described in the article concerns ejected products, not an assertion that the unrecovered share is subsequently recovered.
