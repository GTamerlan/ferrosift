## Research scope

The 180 mm guided central lane on a 600 mm reference belt is a proposed mechanical experiment, not measured sensor coverage. Horizontal XY coils below the belt and upright XZ side coils provide intended complementary orientations. The six functional windings (two transmit and four candidate receive windings) illustrate two transmitters and four receivers; their balance, field patterns and channel arrangement are unoptimized. The stations are sequential, and belt travel does not guarantee fragment association.

Revalis’s wider mission is to recover more copper-bearing material while reducing unnecessary rejection of valuable material across e-waste streams. The proposed contribution is geometry- and pose-conditioned interpretation of directional, frequency-dependent response sequences on irregular layered PCB/e-scrap. Copper-bearing steel composites are one concrete research example, alongside boards and insulated cables. Optical/induction fusion already exists. Comparison with strong single-direction spectral induction and optical baselines on independent fragments and source lots, then collected-output measurement, will determine whether the module adds value. The 2045 horizon is aspirational; space actuation is a separate research program with its own physical and flight gates.
