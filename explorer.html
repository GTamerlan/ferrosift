Scope and open tests

A research apparatus.

Six unoptimized functional windings provide two intended directions. The 180 mm mechanical test lane on a 600 mm host reference is not validated sensing coverage. Installation, frequencies, electrical winding choices, timing and physical performance remain open.

Inspection views omit the near-side housing, covers, winding insulation and surrounding fixtures to reveal the actual hardware. No field simulation, measured induction trace, elemental assay or validated operating command is shown.

Optical, induction and inferred records remain distinct. Belt travel does not prove same-piece association; slip, rotation or overlap can require recheck. Internal wiring, calibration, host interfaces and safe integration require further engineering.

Proposed R&D

Build the evidence.

Electromagnetic instrumentation, motion and optics, material analysis, software, and line integration are proposed workstreams. Test independent fragments and source lots, retain invalid records and measure collected outcomes.

The added direction may fail to earn its hardware and calibration. Retain a simpler design if the evidence supports it.

Example arithmetic

All inputs are assumed.

1,000 t feed × 10% = 100 t contained Cu/year. Recovery at 80% versus 85% gives 5 additional recovered tonnes. 5 t × US$8,000/t − US$20,000 extra operating cost = US$20,000/year.

The payable value is an invented assumption after assumed buyer deductions, not a market quote. This copper-only example excludes changes in other-metal credits, product quality, settlement, unstated route costs, capital, financing and tax. It is not net profit, payback or ROI. The annual feed is an arithmetic input, not rated capacity. CV scores supply no dollars.
