02 / Inductive sensing

Then, we sense.

A second, independent kind of evidence.

A replaceable induction cassette is reserved beneath a nonconductive window. The study tests whether its response adds useful information to RGB. Gap, coverage and interference remain unvalidated; it cannot assay copper or gold.

03 / Sensor fusion + control

Two inputs.
One decision.

Track distance. Bound uncertainty.

The proposed controller joins image and induction observations in encoder coordinates. The final sensing datum is 1250 mm before the lip: 1.25 seconds of travel at the 1 m/s study reference.

Calculated travel is not measured latency. Invalid or overlapping groups need suppressed target ejection and conservative review handling.

04 / Physical sorting

Don’t just identify.
Separate.

A target split, then a review window.

An airjet stage proposes PCB-rich target versus balance. A second gate on the balance chute diverts groups or broad windows to review, after feed inhibit, a verified clear gap and gate-position feedback.

01  PCB-rich target
02  Balance → existing process
03  Balance-window review

The balance gate cannot recover material already ejected into target. Three bins do not imply three-way per-object sorting or a certified safety function.

05 / Verification + improvement

Close the loop.

A correct label is not enough.

Weigh input and every output; separate recognition, tracking and actuator errors. Begin with sparse, qualified 12.5–25 mm and 25–50 mm campaigns. The 50–200 kg/h ramp is a proposed trial, not rated capacity.

Read the calculation and its limitations +

Target metal = annual eligible feed × metal content. Additional metal = target metal × (proposed recovery − baseline recovery). Gross contribution = additional metal × net incremental realization. First-year net = gross contribution − fee − added operations − downtime/quality/error provision − installation. Break-even improvement = total year-one cost ÷ (target metal × net realization), expressed in percentage points.

This simplified target-metal calculation does not model PCB assay, payability, product-by-product settlement or all-output value. Those are necessary for the proposed PCB concept. Use independently measured target-metal mass and buyer specifications. Whole-object mass is not metal mass. Net realization must account for grade, downstream costs and the value already obtained from residue. Replace every assumption with customer data. Throughput loss, financing, taxes and other costs must be added where applicable; do not count an expense twice. Lower recovery is allowed and produces negative results.

Commercial milestone, not an existing contract.
What goes into the first bench?+

Small conveyor; overhead camera and lens; fixed lighting; motor and encoder; optional low-power induction evaluation board; laptop and microcontroller; simple servo chute; clean reference pieces; measurement log; physical guarding and emergency stop.

Planning allowance carried from the working model: $906–$2,484 for sensing plus a slow sorting demonstrator, including 20% contingency. Not a supplier quote. Excludes labor, taxes, shipping, independent assays and industrial qualification.

What are we competing against?+

Established sorters already combine vision, electromagnetic sensing and automated ejection. TOMRA NEW FINDER is a direct example. Ferrosift must earn an advantage in measured performance, cost or integration — not claim this combination is unprecedented. [03]

Where do the safety boundaries sit?+

Begin with approved, clean, battery-free samples. No shredding, unknown dust, charged components or homemade high-speed rotor. Lab staff review power, guarding and emergency stop. Safety acceptance is physical and human-reviewed — not delegated to AI.

The long view · A research thesis

Built on Earth.
An eye on orbit.

First, build something useful here. A network of sorting lines could build a permissioned record of images, electromagnetic responses and verified outcomes — and we want to find out whether any of it transfers beyond recycling.

Next

A permissioned learning network

With partner agreements, study cross-site calibration and reusable models. No customer data is automatically pooled or sold.

2040

Investigate transferable models

Explore research licensing of permitted datasets, estimation methods or simulation tools. Recycling-to-space transfer is unproven.

2045

Explore orbital collaboration

Partner on contactless characterization or detumbling only if separate physics, flight validation and customer demand support it.

Ambition is not a policy deadline.

These years are founder-selected horizons, not enacted cleanup mandates or promised launch dates. Eddy-current detumbling is an existing research area; reducing tumble is not the same as capturing or deorbiting debris. Conveyor data alone does not establish a space capability. [04]
