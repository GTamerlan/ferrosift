REQUIRED DATA BEFORE A TEN-TEST-VIDEO CLAIM
1. At least10 distinct recorded runs with documented rights/consent, provenance
   and a declared sampling rule; independence defined by feed lot/site/day and
   physical pieces, not by slicing one recording into10 files.
2. Preserve original timestamps, frame rate, camera setup and encoder calibration.
   Establish frame/annotation alignment and audit object IDs through occlusion.
3. Define material target labels; independently check them. For recovery/purity
   claims collect weighed/assayed inputs and each output stream, not just boxes.
4. Freeze model/checkpoint hashes, thresholds, NMS and matching before opening
   the held-out evaluation. Keep all results and failure cases; no tuning for wins.
5. Run both frozen implementations on identical frames and report recall,
   precision, misses, false alarms, per-run totals and measured inference latency.
   Keep media playback speed distinct from processing rate and design belt speed.
6. Link the completed later cloud checkpoint/result package locally with hashes
   for reproducibility. Do not retrain to fill a documentation gap.
