One proposed recovery stage. Clear boundaries.

The study places a modular PCB-rich recovery stage after existing ferrous removal and before an eddy-current separator. Feed is already depolluted, liberated, dry and screened. The first campaigns separate 12.5–25 mm and 25–50 mm sieve bands. Whole devices, batteries, hazards, tangles and oversized or heavy pieces are excluded; sieve aperture does not bound wire length or mass.

This is a selected first-product hypothesis, not proof of the most profitable use case. Representative feed, a buyer’s acceptance and assay-based settlement, and comparison with the host’s existing outputs can change the choice.

Two separation decisions, three collection bins

The airjet stage proposes PCB-rich target versus balance. A downstream swing gate affects only the balance path, routing a broad group or conservative window to review. Initial gate changes require feed inhibit, a verified clearing gap and gate-position feedback. The animation demonstrates that sequence but supplies no production timing values.

Chute bounce breaks precise per-item arrival timing. The balance gate cannot recover an uncertain object already ejected into concentrate. Overlapping uncertain groups require upstream suppression of conflicting ejections. A software stop or ordinary review bin is not a certified safety function or battery containment.

Measurements still needed

Sensor gap and nonconductive support, camera height and usable coverage, nozzle pitch and pressure, ejection impulse, splitter geometry, chute slopes and gate transit windows remain adjustable and unvalidated. Separate physical routing success from class recognition, PCB mass recovery, concentrate purity and buyer assay. The optional magnetic research bay is not a demonstrated production module.

Current evidence remains separate

The existing steel/copper YOLO replay shows saved model outputs on its stated evaluation sources. It is not PCB inference and does not validate this mechanical proposal. No physical performance percentages are assigned to the concept.

Research basis

  • TOMRA process context supports PCB recovery before ECS as an existing process option; no Ferrosift yield follows from it.
  • STEINERT KSS CLI establishes commercial precedent for color, 3D and induction applied to PCB concentrates.
  • Umicore’s e-scrap purchasing description provides buyer context; no acceptance, quote or settlement has been obtained.
  • Williams et al., Sensors 2023 illustrates transfer and object-association challenges in aluminium research, not PCB recovery performance.

Concept model and drawings are for engineering discussion. Fabrication release needs selected mechanical/electrical hardware, load and drive calculations, material and actuator trials, and competent machinery-safety review. Guards, ports, clearances and emergency controls shown here are design provisions, not compliance certification.
