On the sorting floor

REAL COMPANIES.
VISIBLE GAPS.

Published examples of material losses and recovery improvements.

01

PNW METAL

Metal recycling · United States

SMALL METALS.
LOST TO LANDFILL.

PNW reported that small metal fragments passed into its residual waste and went to landfill. A new sorting line recovered additional fine wire and expanded its Zorba product offerings fourfold.

Read the case study ↗
02

PREMIER SURPLUS

Electronics recycling · United States

95% RECOVERY.
ON THE FIRST PASS.

Premier reported approximately 95% first-pass material recovery after adding automated sorting. The remaining share needed further recovery; it is not a measure of final landfill losses.
